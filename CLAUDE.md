## What This Is

Sound Typer is a browser app that plays an audio file and "types" text in sync with the music. Louder audio types faster; detected hits (drums, sudden spikes) burst text in and trigger screenshake. Users can load audio files, customize text sources, and tweak how it feels (speed, dynamics, punch, shake, glow) in the Settings modal; the toolbar holds playback, volume, file pickers and the Type/Game mode toggle.

**Game mode** replaces the typewriter with a procedurally generated rhythm game, in one of three styles: bullet hell (dodge patterns fired on the hits), Shapes (Just Shapes & Beats style: dash through telegraphed shapes that land on the beat, rewind to checkpoints on death) or Wave (Geometry Dash wave: hold to fly up, release to fly down, through a scrolling level that turns on the hits).

## Architecture

React 19 + TypeScript + Vite. No router, no state library — single-page app with all state in `App.tsx`.

**Core data flow:** Audio file → decoded `AudioBuffer` → offline analysis in a Web Worker (`TrackAnalysis`: loudness curve, activity, timestamped hits with strength) → each rAF frame, `TypingDriver.step(audiblePosition, dt)` turns the analysis into flow chars + burst chars + hits → `Typewriter.feed()` types and applies shake/glow; `Visualizer.draw()` renders spectrum + timeline.

Key pieces:
- **`src/audio/analysis.ts`** — Pure offline analysis (no DOM; runnable in Bun for testing). STFT → K-weighted loudness normalized per track; SuperFlux-style onset detection on a semitone filterbank with centered adaptive threshold, post-hit masking; hit strength = flux rank OR loudness surprise; per hit lowness and brightness (kick vs hat). Also a brightness curve (spectral centroid), a tonal curve (cheap harmonic/percussive split: median along time vs across bands; high for held notes and pads), and a beat grid: global tempo from the novelty autocorrelation, beats by dynamic programming (Ellis 2007), bar phase (4/4 assumed) by a Viterbi pass over kick, harmony change and onset evidence (`beatTimes`, `beatInBar`). Downbeats are the least reliable part.
- **`src/audio/analyzeBuffer.ts`** / **`analysis.worker.ts`** — Downmix/resample to 22.05 kHz mono via `OfflineAudioContext`, run analysis in a worker.
- **`src/audio/driver.ts`** — `TypingDriver`: time-based (framerate independent) typing from the analysis; handles seeks by resyncing the hit cursor.
- **`src/hooks/useAudioEngine.ts`** — Web Audio API wrapper: load/play/pause/seek/volume. `getPosition()` returns the *audible* position (output latency compensated via `getOutputTimestamp`).
- **`src/hooks/useAnimationLoop.ts`** — rAF loop calling `onFrame(dt)`.
- **`src/components/Typewriter.tsx`** — Displays typed text via imperative `feed(frame, dt)`. Spring-physics screenshake, hit glow (`--hit` CSS var), word snapping on strong hits.
- **`src/components/Controls.tsx`** — Toolbar: file chips (drag-and-drop), transport, volume. Wraps to two rows below 768px.
- **`src/components/Visualizer.tsx`** — Canvas: log-frequency spectrum + scrolling timeline of intensity and hits.
- **`src/components/Settings.tsx`** — Modal for all tuning: typing feel (`FeelPrefs`, stored in `sound-typer-prefs`), game difficulty, effects and layout (`SettingsState`, stored in `sound-typer-settings`; also holds the current mode), reset to defaults.

Game mode (`src/game/`; everything except the renderers is DOM-free and runnable in Bun). Three styles behind one `GameEngine` interface (`types.ts`), picked by `SettingsState.gameStyle`:
- **`common.ts`** — Seeded RNG (per track, same for every difficulty), section splitting (~6–13 s, snapped to strong hits; Bullet hell), song structure (`songParts`: the intensity curve fit with flat parts, boundaries moved onto the sharpest change and the hit there; Shapes and Wave), sliding per-second budget, shake spring, canvas setup. Play area zoom: Shapes and Bullet hell scale their play area around its centre so attacks cover about the same share of it (`Coverage`, `scaleFor`: from 0.4 = 2.5x zoom when little is going on to ~1.1 when packed; grows fast, shrinks slowly, `easeScale`). Shapes precomputes it from the chart (`zoomCurve`, looking ~0.75 s ahead, and no single hazard spans more than 40% of the area), so it's a pure function of song time; Bullet hell follows the live bullets (each with some dodging room, culled only at the widest area so a small area doesn't feed on itself). Hazards and emitters land in the area as it is when they appear, the player is pushed in by its edges, the camera always shows exactly the area (`GameEngine.scale`, also used to map the pointer), all scaled by the `cameraZoom` setting. `hitWeights` rates every hit: `prom` (how much it stands out from the hits within ±4 s, so quiet passages still have standouts; decides whether/what spawns) and `heft` (strength × intensity; decides how big, fast and wide the attack is).
- **`bullets/`** — Bullet hell. `chart.ts` schedules patterns from emitters per section: pellets (weak hits), aimed fans (crisp), rings (bassy/strong), telegraphed lasers (strongest), spiral streams in loud sections. `sim.ts` spawns them as the audio position passes, advances bullets by audio time, handles collisions and grazes (sparks only). `render.ts` draws (sprite-cached glowing bullets, charge-up telegraphs).
- **`shapes/`** — Just Shapes & Beats style. `chart.ts` splits the song into `songParts`, each with a role (calm, main, build, drop, break; from its song-relative level and the jump from the part before) and a colour per role (the renderer eases into it and washes the screen on part changes); the last 3–8 s before a drop is its build (only standout hits, mostly a trail, half budget, the arena squeezes in to `BUILD_SQUEEZE` and the zoom look-ahead stops at the drop so it bursts open on the beat) and a drop opens with a cross of bars bursting into a ring of shots. Themes (field, bars, steps, walls, spinner, rain, grid) come in runs of bars (`shapesRuns`, on the beat grid; 2 s bars where there's none): a run goes on while its bars sound alike (`Feel`: density, kick/hat/heavy shares, tonal, intensity) and ends when the music changes (`RUN_CHANGE`, or at a 4-bar phrase once it drifted, or after `RUN_MAX_BARS`), never crossing a section, so attacks follow the music rather than a timer. Each run's theme is weighted by its feel (accented → bars, kicks and punchy → walls, steady beat → steps, hats → rain, sustained → spinner, steady kicks → grid; drops lean on the big themes) and a run that sounds like an earlier one (`RUN_RECALL`) reuses its theme, unless that theme just played or was used `RECENT_MAX` times in the last `RECENT_RUNS` runs (each recent use also makes rolling a theme less likely). Within a run every hit is a voice (kick: bassy, hat: bright, else snare) and the theme gives each voice its own attack, so no one attack takes over the screen: e.g. in steps the march is the kick line (beats 1 and 3 when a run has few kicks; it pauses when they do) while snares pop up, hats fire volleys and heavy hits slam a bar across. Cooldowns that are about the music count in beats (seekers `seekerBeats`, ray bursts, build volleys, walls `wallBars` with a `WALL_GAP_MIN` floor); the ones that guarantee walking time stay in seconds. Walls only come on the heaviest hit of a bar; the steps march and the bomb trail turn on downbeats. Hits become hazards that warn, go solid on the beat, then shrink; every hazard's geometry is a pure function of time (`geometry()`), shared by collisions and drawing. Bars across the arena are the main element; small hazards come in predictable patterns (bomb trails, shot volleys), pop-ups are placed apart, and homing is limited to small, spaced-out seekers plus targeted bars (they lock onto the player when the warning starts). Every hazard warns at least long enough to walk out of it (`warnFor`), so a dash is never forced. An idle fallback fills silences longer than `idleMax` when the music has hits. Hits are classified by `prom` and every hazard's size, width, speed, lifetime and budget cost scale with `heft` (heavy hits, only from intensity `HEAVY_INTEN` up, get walls, crosses, ray bursts and pop-ups that burst into rings of shots; homing bars only from `TARGET_INTEN` up). Walls lurch forward on the beats of their sweep (`kicks`, `wallProgress`), the heaviest come from both sides at once with gaps lined up. Steps lanes end just before the march's next beat (`stepSlack` before it), so the march lands on the beat without two lanes ever being solid; in dense streams a lesser hit yields to a clearly bigger one right behind it. The spawn budget and projectile speeds scale with the music's intensity (`intensityAt` in `common.ts`, the typewriter's curve); no single obstacle covers more than half the arena. The renderer is intensity-driven too (speed streaks, camera punch on strong hits, bass thump, pumping background). Spinner beams follow a sampled `BeamPath` (drifting pivot, loudness-driven spin, kicks on hits) but only live while a sustained sound holds (`beamHolds`: the tonal curve, averaged over a beat, above the run's average with hysteresis; dips up to a beat don't end a hold; at least a bar unless the level really drops, at most `BEAM_BARS` bars, leaving on a beat; a beat apart, a bar after one that hit the cap); spinners only fit runs with real sustain (`SPINNER_TONAL`) and at least one hold. Big hits fire ray bursts from the pivot. Grid: the bar's heaviest hit flashes half a checkerboard and the next bar's the other half, then `GRID_REST_BARS` empty bars (`grid`; warned to walk half a cell, walking time between flashes; not on easy; doesn't count towards the zoom's coverage), kicks and snares pop up. Rests (`findRests`: the loudness drops out for 0.2–3 s and comes back on a hit): every hazard solid when the music stops freezes (`freeze`; geometry runs on the hazard's own clock, `localTime`, and its `end` moves back), nothing spawns in the silence, and the return hit snaps pop-ups warned from the start of the silence (the first bursting into a ring; a drop's slam replaces it); the renderer stops the background and dims it, then flashes on the return. `sim.ts`: movement, dash (invulnerable, cooldown), health, checkpoints; running out of health asks Game to rewind the song (`takeRewind`), which doesn't count as a seek.
- **`wave/`** — Geometry Dash wave. `chart.ts` lays an ideal path at the wave's slope that turns on hits (gap and strength threshold scale with intensity), sampled at `RATE` per second together with world x (`xs`, scroll speed per section), then builds around it with clearance: tunnels (`top`/`bot` walls, height = `r + 2·vy·window`, so the difficulty's timing window is explicit), looser spiked corridors with saws outside turns or block columns with a gap, mini sections (slope 2), spam sections (bursts of `SPAM_LEN` s with `SPAM_BREAK` s of plain tunnel between: a straight or gently sloped corridor only `spamStroke` seconds of flight tall, so staying inside takes ~1.5/2/2.6 taps per second (at least `SPAM_MIN_FREE` units of room, more on slopes); the ideal path turns on beats near its centre line and is forced to turn at the corridor's edge), gravity-flipped parts (`flip`: holding flies down; normal/hard, after 20 s, preferably on intensity jumps; the sim inverts input and the renderer draws a gravity portal). Sections come from the song's structure (`songParts`, in `common.ts`; neighbours that get the same speed stage merge), each part gets a speed stage (`SPEED_STAGES`) from its intensity on a song-relative scale, and the busiest intense parts (the drops) go mini; long parts are split only to change obstacles, picked by the music's character (bassy → pillars, heavy accents → saws, dense → spam, calm → tunnel). Turns use `prom` (calm parts take long sweeps through roomier tunnels); heavier turns bite teeth into the outside wall (keeping 3/4 of the timing slack) and size the saws. Walls are filtered so they never close in faster than the wave flies (fair tunnel entrances). The level is static, so seeking needs no replay. `sim.ts` integrates the wave by audio time with sub-steps; a hit puts it back on the path; health/checkpoints/rewinds work like Shapes. The level is open (no floor or ceiling; the path is never bounded and takes any hit after flying straight `MAX_STRAIGHT`). `render.ts`: the camera follows the wave vertically and zooms with the speed stage (`ZOOM_SLOW`..`ZOOM_FAST`); flat zinc look, one color per speed stage, zigzag trail, beat lines, speed portals and meter.
- **`src/components/Game.tsx`** — Canvas + input (keyboard, mouse, touch incl. dash gestures) + HUD + start/pause/results overlays (style picker on the Ready card). Reuses a `TypingDriver` only to get music hits for shake/glow. App calls `frame()` each rAF, `start()` before playback and `finish()` when the song ends.

**Persistence:** Three localStorage keys (`sound-typer-prefs`, `sound-typer-text-source`, `sound-typer-settings`) store user preferences, custom text and settings. Game mode has no score: the HUD shows health and hits, and the end card shows hits taken and rewinds.
